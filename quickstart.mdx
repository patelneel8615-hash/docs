## Create Registration types

Creating custom **Company Registration Types** is the first step to tailoring your organization's profile within the website. These registration types are essential for displaying company details on reports.

  src="/images/registration-type-create-2-1.png"

1. Locate the **plus icon (+)** on the _Company Registration Types_ section of your Settings page. This icon is usually visible near the list of existing registration types. _(Refer to the screenshot below for the exact location of the plus icon.)_
2. Click the **plus icon (+)** to open a dialog.
3. Enter a name and description.
4. Save Registration Type by clicking the save button.

Once created, these registration types are fully customizable and unique to your organization. You can add, edit, or remove them anytime, allowing flexibility as your company evolves.

## **Company Details -Setting Up an Organization**

**Company Details** - After you have created your company registration types, the next important step is to add your company’s details.

**Company Registration** - The registration section displays a list of all the previously created company registration types. This convenient interface allows you to include or exclude the registration from documents by using simple checkboxes.
